# AlternativeTo listing copy

## List as alternative to

Expensify, QuickBooks, Zoho Expense, Shoeboxed, FreshBooks

## Short description (one line)

Receipt and mileage tracker for freelancers: the receipt files itself from a photo, an email, or your FastMail inbox, and you just approve it.

## Full description

Expense is a web-based expense tracker built for freelancers and self-employed people who file their own taxes (Schedule C / 1099) and hate data entry.

Where traditional expense software gives you a form per receipt, Expense treats capture as the app's job, not yours:

- Photos, screenshots, and PDFs are read by OCR plus a language model: merchant, amount, and date extracted, category suggested from IRS Schedule C lines, with suggestions improving as it learns your own spending history.
- A private email intake address turns forwarded receipts or emailed photos into drafted expenses.
- A FastMail account can be connected so receipts are filed automatically as they arrive. Unlike most alternatives, Expense does not ask for Gmail access; it authenticates to FastMail with a revocable token.
- Duplicate receipts are detected by image hash, so double submissions never double-count.
- Foreign currency receipts are converted at the official ECB reference rate for the purchase date.

Beyond receipts: mileage logging from an interactive map at the IRS rate for the drive date, and bank statement reconciliation that matches uploaded statements against logged expenses to surface forgotten deductions. An MCP server lets AI assistants (Claude and any MCP client) query and log expenses in plain English.

Expense is free while the app is under 100 users; afterward a paid plan applies with a permanent free tier (25 invoices a month). No ads, no credit card required, and no data selling: receipts belong to the account that uploaded them.

## Reasons to switch (comments)

The one-click-per-receipt workflow: OCR and AI do the typing, you approve. Most alternatives make you do the data entry with a camera attached.
The privacy trade: no Gmail password required. FastMail users get automatic import with a self-issued, revocable token; everyone else gets a private email intake address.
The reconciliation feature matches your bank statement against logged expenses and finds the deductions you forgot, something the big-name tools treat as an enterprise feature.
